About the Crawler

An interactive digital spider

An animated black widow-inspired spider with a glossy, rounded abdomen, a fused head-and-thorax shield, a narrow pedicel, and eight articulated legs. Its dorsal surface uses restrained red accents; the familiar widow hourglass belongs on the underside. It explores freely, follows your pointer, and investigates stillness with alternating front-leg probes. It is rendered at a smaller scale, like a small creature living on the page.
